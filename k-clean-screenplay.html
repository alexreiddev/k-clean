Series Bible

Three people. Three rooms. One smiling bag of fertilizer.
The corruption isn't hidden — it's certified.

Color Grade
Cynical Teal. High-end. Sharp shadows. Government office cleanliness with a green-tinted rot underneath.
Soundscape
A deep, distorted synthesizer playing a slow Vanchippattu (boat song) rhythm. Each episode is one oar stroke.
The Anchor
A neon-green K-Clean Organic Fertilizer bag with a smiling Earth logo. Present in every episode. Holds something different each time.
I
Episode One

The Harvest

Character
SUKU · 60s · retired teacher
Location
Backyard garden · Thiruvananthapuram
Runtime
~22 minutes
FADE IN:
EXT. SUKU'S BACKYARD GARDEN — DAY
Lush. Banana leaves throw jagged shadows. Somewhere, a pressure cooker whistles three houses down.
EXTREME CLOSE-UP. A trembling, elderly finger. A jagged, bleeding cut. The finger holds a dull, yellow metallic PEBBLE, rinsed under a tap. The water runs pink.
WIDE — SAME
SUKU (60s, threadbare "Janakeeya" cotton shirt, the kind of man neighbours call "Sir") crouches at his garden tap. Beside him — propped against the wall — a half-empty K-CLEAN BAG. Neon green. The smiling Earth logo grinning right at us.
His eyes lift to a framed CERTIFICATE OF APPRECIATION on the porch. The kind given for civic duty.
He looks back at the pebble. Pulls a small whetstone from his pocket. Scratches.
The pebble is SOFT. 24-karat.
Suku stares. He turns slowly toward the bag. Reaches in. Past the dark compost. His hand finds something heavy.
He pulls out a vacuum-sealed plastic packet. Dirty on the outside. Unmistakable by weight.
Insert — CU The "BATCH NUMBER" printed on the K-Clean bag. It is not a number. It is a string of digits:

8.4912° N, 76.9502° E

A GPS coordinate. A beach.
Suku's face moves through three things in two seconds: confusion. recognition. fear.
The GATE LATCH clicks open.
EXT. GATE — CONTINUOUS
A white SUV. GOVERNMENT OF KERALA board on the dashboard. The DRIVER steps out — 40s, safari suit, the shoulders of a commando. He doesn't ring the bell. He walks in.
DRIVER
(calm, almost echoing)
Suku Sir... are you happy with the yield? The Minister was asking.
Suku PALMS the gold into his trouser pocket. Blood from his finger smears the inside of the cotton. He stands. Smiles the way a teacher smiles at an inspector.
SUKU
(too quickly)
Yes. It's... pure.
DRIVER
(smiles back, doesn't blink)
Good. We're here for the refill.
The Driver's smile widens. It does not reach his eyes.
CUT TO BLACK.
The PING of a smartwatch.
— End of Episode One —
II
Episode Two

The Protocol

Character
ANJALI · 30s · PR fixer
Location
High-rise glass office
Runtime
~21 minutes
FADE IN:
INT. ANJALI'S OFFICE — HIGH FLOOR — DAY
Wall-to-wall glass. The city of Thiruvananthapuram laid out below like a circuit board. The same pressure cooker whistles — somewhere — barely audible.
A muted TV is bolted to a black wall. News ticker:
News ticker "BREAKING: 2 KILOS OF GOLD SEIZED AT AIRPORT — MAJOR VICTORY FOR CUSTOMS."
ANJALI (30s, sharp, the kind of person who irons their own collar) is laughing. Not amused — pleased.
She sits at a glass desk. Black coffee. A plate of Pazham Pori — banana fritters — which she is eating with a silver fork and knife. The grease will not touch her.
ANJALI
(into a burner phone)
The bait worked. Customs is busy posing for photos with the two kilos. Tell the boys to move the other two hundred through the green channels.
She opens her laptop. A map of the state. Covered in neon-green dots.
She clicks one.
Insert — Screen WARD 4 — SUKU (TEACHER). STATUS: ACTIVE. SEAL: COMPROMISED.
Her smile flickers.
On her desk: a framed photograph. ANJALI, mid-handshake at a ribbon-cutting. Beside her — beaming — the same DRIVER from Episode One. This is not a driver.
ANJALI
(into phone)
Wait. Ward 4 had a seal leak. The old man found a nugget.
Pause. She listens.
ANJALI (cont'd)
No. Don't clean him. He's a teacher. People like him.
ANJALI (cont'd)
(quieter)
We don't kill icons. We recruit them.
She drags a file across her desktop. Drops it into a folder labelled "AMBASSADORS."
ANJALI
Promotion time.
Outside, an AMBULANCE siren wails past her glass tower. The bag of fertilizer it is carrying is the same neon green.
The siren bleeds into a low, distorted Vanchippattu drone.
CUT TO BLACK.
— End of Episode Two —
III
Episode Three · Finale

The Ambassador

Characters
SECRETARY · SUKU
Location
VIP cabin · 03:14 AM
Runtime
~24 minutes
FADE IN:
INT. VIP CABIN — STATE GUEST HOUSE — 03:14 AM
Sterile. Luxury. The kind of room where decisions are made and forgotten. A wall clock — silent — reads 03:14.
SUKU sits across a polished mahogany table. He is in a crisp new silk shirt — the High Command kind. His finger is wrapped in a clean, premium medical dressing. He looks ten years younger and a thousand years older.
Opposite him: THE SECRETARY. Late 50s. Linen-soft voice. The man who signs nothing and decides everything.
The Secretary slides a tablet across the table.
SECRETARY
(softly)
Suku Sir. Your grandson's admission to the London School of Economics is confirmed. Full scholarship.
SECRETARY (cont'd)
Courtesy of the K-Clean Youth Foundation.
Suku looks at the screen. His eyes are wet. On the table — between them — a white envelope. Unmarked.
The Secretary rises. Walks to a K-CLEAN BAG in the corner. Same neon green. Same smiling Earth. He opens it.
Inside — past the compost layer — neatly stacked rows of SILVER BRICKS. Stamped. Untraceable.
SECRETARY
People think we're cleaning the streets, Suku Sir.
SECRETARY (cont'd)
(turning to face him)
We're cleaning the money. And you — you are going to be the face of our honesty.
INSERT — THE CONTRACT
A single page. Embossed. The header reads:
Document "OFFICE OF THE CHIEF VIGILANCE OFFICER — K-CLEAN INITIATIVE"

Appointee: Sri. Suku [REDACTED]
Effective: Immediately.
Suku takes the pen. His bandaged finger trembles. He signs.
He is no longer a gardener. He is a shield.
CAMERA pans down to the bandaged finger. Pulls back. The whole room is teal-cold. The K-Clean bag in the corner is the only thing that has any colour left.
The Secretary taps his watch. 03:14 AM.
SECRETARY
Shift change. Let's go to the press meet.
Off-screen: the sound of a crowd. Building. Chanting — "SUKU SIR! SUKU SIR!"
INT. PRESS HALL — CONTINUOUS — STILL 03:14 AM
Flashbulbs. Microphones in his face. Suku adjusts his glasses. He looks past the cameras. Past the audience. Past us.
SUKU
(steady, like a teacher)
It's pure.
The same two words. The same voice. A different room. A different man.
CUT TO:
EXT. SUKU'S BACKYARD GARDEN — DAY (EPISODE ONE)
The trembling finger. The rinsing pebble. The pink water.
SUKU (V.O.)
It's pure.
FADE TO GREEN.
— End of Series · The Loop Closes —
Structural Note

The Loop

"It's pure" is said twice. Once by an honest man who has just discovered a lie. Once by a corrupt man performing the truth. The same words. The same voice. Three episodes apart.

The Harvest
"It's pure."

The Protocol
"Promotion time."

For The Director

The Oh Shit Moments

01

The Wardrobe Tells the Story

Suku wears two shirts across the three episodes. The audience never sees him change. They feel the change.

Episode 1
Faded "Janakeeya" cotton shirt. People's man. Worn at the elbows.
Episode 3
High Command silk. Sharp. Unwrinkled. Foreign-tailored.
02

The Hidden Iconography

Every government vehicle in the background of every episode carries a small green sticker on its rear bumper. The audience won't catch it on first watch. By the third episode they will start counting.

Marks a "safe vehicle." A vehicle no one will ever stop. A vehicle that delivers fertilizer.

03

The Sound Bridge

A pressure cooker whistle threads through every episode. Three houses down in Episode 1. Down the hall in Episode 2. Inside the cabin in Episode 3. By the finale, it is the loudest thing on the soundtrack — and no one in the scene reacts.

04

The Smiling Earth

The K-Clean logo never looks away from the camera. The face is on the bag in every episode, framed slightly off-centre — so it always feels like it is watching the actor, not the audience. It is the only character that sees all three rooms.

The Social Media Hook

Scan the Frame

The final frame of the playlist holds a real-looking QR code.

k-clean.gov.in.fake

It leads to a fabricated Kerala-government website. Success stories. Citizen of the Month. A photo of an old man holding a fertilizer bag, smiling. The viewer is meant to wonder if their local award-winner is in the chain.
